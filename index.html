How it works & honest limits

The optimiser builds a linear programme — one variable per ingredient (its % of the blend), the blend total fixed at 100%, one linear constraint per target — and solves it exactly with a two-phase simplex method (Bland’s rule, so it cannot cycle). Cost and proximate composition (protein, fat, moisture, carbs) genuinely do blend linearly, so for the numbers you enter, the answer is the true least-cost blend.

What LP cannot do: it is not a sensory or texture model. Flavour, bite, colour and processing behaviour enter only through the numeric constraints you set (bounds, ratios, group shares). Preset compositions and prices are illustrative teaching values — commercial formulation needs verified lab composition for your actual raw materials and current supplier prices. If the solver reports infeasible, no blend of the listed ingredients within their bounds can meet all targets at once; the app shows which targets fail simple achievability checks to help you find the clash. Everything runs in this page — no server, no account, no data leaves your browser.
